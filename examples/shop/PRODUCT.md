# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Inferred (the user skipped the interview): the people who watch the Leakproof demo video, meaning hackathon judges (TestMu AI engineers) and web developers. They need to believe this is a real, well-made shop, so that "it looks perfect but leaks" lands.

## Product Purpose
Driftwood Supply is a fictional independent home-goods shop. It exists only as the scan target for Leakproof. It must look finished and trustworthy while hiding six planted leaks that a screenshot cannot see.

## Positioning
Small-batch objects for the home (mugs, throws, lamps, trays), each sold like a piece in a museum shop: named, described by material, and numbered.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Pages: home (hero, product list, add to cart, popular picks), sign in, checkout.
- The planted leaks must stay exactly as coded: the slow unsized hero image, the late promo banner, the 500 from recommendations, the analytics TypeError on add to cart, the localStorage token and key, the staging fetch on checkout, and the insecure session cookie.
- Plain CSS, Next.js App Router, production build.

## Evidence on Hand
No real product photography. Product names and prices are fictional demo content, and the footer says so.

## Product Principles
- It must look like a shop someone really runs, not a template.
- Every leak stays invisible on the surface.
- Demo content is honest: fictional, and labelled as a demo.
